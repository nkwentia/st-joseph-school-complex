About ST JATEC

ST JATEC Modernises Technical Education

Welcome to St. Joseph's Foundation Anglo-Saxon Technical College (ST JATEC), a purely English technical college in Yaoundé IV Sub-division. Established in 2015, we offer professionalized technical education with a focus on building individual skills for economic empowerment at any level. Our students learn practical, marketable trades that allow them to be self-reliant — without depending on scarce government employment.
