St Joseph's Foundation School Complex is an independent, non-partisan day school serving boys and girls from age 2 to 18. It is made up of four schools under one roof:

The Bilingual Nursery and Primary School was established in 2007. The Anglo-Saxon Secondary School (St JASS) opened in 2012 at parents' request to champion English-medium education. The Anglo-Saxon Technical High School (ST JATEC), established in 2015, trains students in practical vocational skills to become self-reliant.

Today, St Joseph's ranks among the best schools in the region, offering a unique continuous education pathway from a bilingual nursery and primary school to an Anglo Saxon high school and an Anglo Saxon technical college — and recording outstanding GCE/TVEE examinations results.
